The House

A mill that still listens to the loom.

Four generations of weavers, one principle: let the fibre lead. We spin, dye and weave in small lots, so every bolt carries the grain of the hand that made it. No seconds, no shortcuts, no synthetic filler.

Master-woven

Each pattern signed off by a head weaver before it leaves the floor.

Low-impact dye
